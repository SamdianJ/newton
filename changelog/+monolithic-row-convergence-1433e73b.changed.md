Judge experimental `SolverMonolithic` convergence per residual row in physical units: every row must lie within the largest of an absolute target, a relative tolerance on its block's step-start residual (1e-6 in FP64, 1e-4 in FP32) and 32 times the precision's machine epsilon times that row's rounding magnitude. Shell bending and cable bending and twist rows bound their rounding from the operands of their strains. Small local loads are no longer accepted unresolved because the residual of a large model was averaged. Linear solves use one global inexact-Newton forcing term.
