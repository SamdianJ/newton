Step every experimental `SolverMonolithic` scene through one producer pipeline. Scenes no longer need an articulation or tet: rigid-only and material-only models construct directly, so remove any inert auxiliary link or zero-mass tet that was added only to satisfy the constructor, and use `create_control()` for joint-free models. Newton directions now retry a fixed regularization schedule after zero lambda; material scenes scale articulation, rigid and tet rows by their dynamic diagonal. Material-scene checkpoints carry a new configuration identity and must be recreated.
